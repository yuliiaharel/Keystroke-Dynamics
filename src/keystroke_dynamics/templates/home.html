About this project

Keystroke Dynamics authenticates you partly by how you type, not just what you type. It has two parts:

  1. Create a profile — register an account and type a sample passage. The app learns the timing between your key presses.
  2. Authenticate — log in and type again. Your password is still checked; your typing pattern is an additional signal.
Get started Check authenticity

What is keystroke dynamics?

Keystroke dynamics is the detailed timing of when each key is pressed and released as someone types. See How It Works for the actual method this app uses and its measured accuracy.
